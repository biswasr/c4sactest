The only task of science that really counts

“I consider science as an integrating part of our endeavour to answer the one great philosophical question which embraces all others, the one that Plotinus expressed by his brief: "Who are we?"" And more than that, I consider this not one of the tasks, but the task of science, the only one that really counts.” - Erwin Schrödinger

Erwin Schrödinger was a Nobel prize winning Austrian physicist. His contributions to quantum physics have significantly influenced our understanding of the fundamental nature of reality. [Biography]
